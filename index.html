Interlinear Lyrics

A Chrome extension. It shows a Russian translation under every line of the song lyrics in Spotify, and the tempo of the playing track above its button.

Version 1.9 · requires Chrome 138 or newer · about 150 KB

Download ZIP

What it does

  • A translation under each lineWhen the lyrics are open in Spotify, a Russian translation appears under every English line, in step with the highlighted line.
  • Medallion buttonBottom right of the page. Dark medallion: translation off. Glowing medallion: translation on. A tooltip shows on hover.
  • Track tempo (BPM)Above the medallion it shows the tempo of the playing song, for example “103 BPM”. The data comes from SonoVault and needs their free API key.

Installation

  1. Click “Download ZIP” and unzip it. You get a folder named spotify-ru-lyrics.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on “Developer mode” at the top right.
  4. Click “Load unpacked” and choose the unzipped folder.
  5. Open open.spotify.com, play a song and open its lyrics (the microphone button in the player).
  6. Click the medallion at the bottom right. On first use Chrome downloads the Russian language pack once.
  7. For the tempo: get a free key at sonovault.now and confirm your email address. Then click the badge above the medallion that asks for a key, and paste the key in.

Good to know

  • The translation is machine-madeIt is done by Chrome’s built-in translator on your own computer, and the lyrics are not sent anywhere. Figurative lyrics can come out too literal.
  • Title and artist go to the tempo databaseOnly to show the BPM: the extension sends the track title and artist name to SonoVault, together with your key. The key is stored in your Chrome. The free key allows 1,000 lookups per month, and every track is looked up only once, so each person should get their own key.
  • It can breakThe extension reads the Spotify page, and Spotify may change it. Then the translation or the tempo stops showing until the extension is updated.
  • The tempo is approximateSome songs are not in the database or have not been analysed yet, and for songs with a changing tempo it is a single averaged number.
  • Russian labelsThe extension’s own labels, such as the button tooltip and the badge messages, are in Russian for now.
